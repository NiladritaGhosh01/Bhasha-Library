## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an author and reader sanctuary celebrating literary craftsmanship. The identity bridges Indian folk classical art—specifically Pattachitra's narrative linework, botanical ornamentation, and intricate framing—with European Renaissance manuscript illumination, characterized by warm chiaroscuro, gilded framing, and rhythmic classical typography.

### Audience & Mood
The experience targets bibliophiles, independent authors, literary translators, and archival collectors. The emotional tone balances scholarly gravitas with tactile warmth. Interactions should feel deliberate, organic, and physically grounded, avoiding sterile corporate flatness in favor of parchment-like warmth, illuminated accents, and responsive tactile delight.

### Visual Style
- **Manuscript Illumination & Folio Craft:** Layouts behave like bounded classical manuscripts. Decorative corner rosettes, vegetal framing fillets, and drop-caps punctuate prolonged reading sessions.
- **Renaissance Chiaroscuro & Pattachitra Geometry:** High contrast between deep pigment inks and sunlit vellum, supported by fine-line filigree, arched framing devices, and medallion badges.
- **Tactile Micro-Affordances:** Despite the antique aesthetic, interactive elements possess an organic, bouncy physical feedback—depressing like hand-cast lead type or sealing wax when pressed.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in historic mineral pigments, organic dyes, and antique bookbinding materials:

### Light Mode (Default: Sunlit Vellum)
- **Primary Ink (`#4A1521`):** Deep Cherry Burgundy. Serves as primary text, primary button fills, key structural dividers, and high-emphasis icons. Sub-shades `#3B1019` and `#2E0B12` provide heightened contrast for display titles and dense prose.
- **Secondary / Gilded Accent (`#D4AF37`):** Antique Gold. Used for ornamental borders, illuminated foil highlights, badges, and focus halos.
- **Tertiary / Mineral Earth (`#C85A32`):** Terracotta Ochre. Applied to bookmark flags, active progress indicators, pull-quote margins, and secondary callouts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF6EE` to `#F7EFE1`):** Vanilla Cream and Warm Parchment. Base canvas and layered panel card tones evoking aged handmade paper.
- **Surface Borders (`rgba(74, 21, 33, 0.14)`):** Fine iron-gall ink rulings framing containers.

### Dark Mode (Nocturne / Crushed Velvet Binding)
- **Primary Canvas (`#230A0F` to `#1A070B`):** Deep Cherry Black / Vintage Morocco Leather.
- **Primary Text (`#FBF6EE`):** Crisp Vanilla Cream with parchment softness to reduce glare.
- **Gilded Borders & Accents (`#E1BF54`):** Soft luminous gold for corner motifs and active states.
- **Secondary Surfaces (`#2F1017`):** Elevated reading folios and navigation decks.
- **Tertiary Highlight (`#DC6B43`):** Warm terracotta amber for status indicators and active tabs.

## Typography

The typography pays tribute to early movable type and classical illuminated folios, pairing historical display proportions with rhythmic long-form readability.

### Type Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Headlines & Display (EB Garamond):** Used for story titles, volume headings, and prominent banner statements. Set with generous line heights and subtle negative tracking on large scales. For Bengali script matching, pair with an equivalent high-contrast Renaissance/literary Bengali serif (such as *Noto Serif Bengali* or *Tiro Bangla*) tuned to match the optical weight and classical x-height of EB Garamond.
- **Reading Body (Source Serif 4):** Chosen for its sturdy serifs, generous apertures, and exceptional stability across long-form reading interfaces. Body line heights are deliberately open (`1.6` to `1.625`) to mimic traditional book layout leading.
- **Labels & Metatext:** Styled in uppercase or small-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.1em`), providing an archival rubrication feel reminiscent of book chapter headers, cataloguing numbers, and folio margins.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the balanced margins of classical manuscripts and traditional print proportions (van de Graaf canon).

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Centered max-width reader canvas (optimal reading column capped at `72ch` or `760px`). Catalogs and multi-panel dashboards follow a 12-column fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` margins to establish generous border space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margin. Side rails collapse into an archival bottom dock.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing relies on a strict 8px/4px base scale:
- Components use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for internal icon-to-label gaps.
- Medium paddings `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) govern card interiors and reader controls.
- Macro spacing `space-xl` (40px) and double-multiples frame illuminated section breaks and book chapters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use cold drop shadows; instead, it relies on layered physical parchment, ink outlines, and gilded chiaroscuro.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Ledger):** The main window `#FBF6EE`, featuring a subtle fibrous vellum grain texture.
- **Tier 1 (Mounted Folio):** Cards, chapters, and reader panels sit on `#F7EFE1`, bound by a crisp hairline border (`1px solid rgba(74, 21, 33, 0.16)`).
- **Tier 2 (Raised Plaques & Popovers):** Elevated overlays receive a dual border treatment: an outer `1px solid rgba(74, 21, 33, 0.18)` and a soft warm ambient halo: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(74, 21, 33, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 21, 33, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Gilded Modal & Illuminations):** Reserved for reader settings, glossary sheets, and archival search modals. Framed with a 1.5px gold-tinted ruling (`#D4AF37`) backed by a translucent burgundy scrim (`rgba(46, 11, 18, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectural, slightly softened corner philosophy (`roundedness: 1` — base radius of `0.25rem` / 4px, `rounded-lg` of `0.5rem` / 8px).

### Border & Motifs
- **Tailored Corners:** Corners are kept slightly softened rather than pill-shaped or purely razor-sharp, mimicking hand-trimmed, deckle-edged book boards.
- **Pattachitra Fillet Insets:** Featured cards and reader viewports feature an internal pseudo-border inset by `4px` or `6px` with subtle corner brackets (corner knots or lotus petal motifs) drawn in hairline gold or muted ink.
- **Arched Portals:** Author avatars, spotlight book covers, and category medallions adopt Renaissance arch or classic circular medallion silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Affordances
- **Primary Buttons:** Rich Cherry Burgundy background (`#4A1521`), Vanilla text (`#FBF6EE`), with a subtle inner top gold hairline highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`).
  - *Micro-Interaction:* Tactile, bouncy spring mechanics. Hover initiates a gentle lift (`transform: translateY(-2px)` with warm shadow expansion). Active state triggers an elastic, satisfying depression (`transform: translateY(1px) scale(0.98)` via cubic-bezier `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`).
- **Secondary Buttons (Gold Filigree):** Parchment background (`#F7EFE1`) bounded by an Antique Gold border (`1.5px solid #D4AF37`) and Cherry text. Hover washes the surface in a pale gold tint (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`).
- **Tertiary / Text Actions:** Underlined with a delicate decorative flourish or dotted rule that solidifies into an Indian classical scroll stroke on hover.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Fields are rendered as warm vellum writing surfaces (`#FDFBF7`) encased in a `1px` border of `rgba(74, 21, 33, 0.25)`.
- Focused state replaces the default ring with a sharp Antique Gold double-rule or `0 0 0 2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` halo, evoking illuminated book margins.
- Placeholder text is set in italicized `Source Serif 4` at 50% opacity.

### Reader & Manuscript Cards
- **Book / Manuscript Cards:** Outer frame includes a double-rule border (outer 1px ink line, inner 0.5px faint gold line). Book spines and covers feature subtle chiaroscuro gradients along their left binding edge.
- **Reading Canvas:** Uncluttered textual column framed by subtle chapter headers, leaf-motif page dividers, and reading progress represented as a slender Terracotta Ochre ribbon along the top edge.

### Chips, Tags & Badges
- Genre tags and language badges (e.g., Bengali, Sanskrit, English) are styled as archival stamped seals: small caps, `0.25rem` corner radius, tinted with Ochre or Gold ink wash (`rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.08)`), framed with a hairline border.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes are small squares with clipped or softened corners. When checked, they fill with Deep Burgundy and display a hand-drawn quill-like checkmark rendered in Antique Gold.
- Radio buttons feature a concentric medallion ring with an inner gold jewel dot when active.

### Extended Components
- **Drop-Caps:** Elaborate initial capital letters rendered in EB Garamond with illuminated floral/Pattachitra vine overlays for long-form essays and story opening chapters.
- **Ornamental Section Dividers:** SVGs featuring stylized floral crests, conch, or palmette arabesques placed between chapters.
